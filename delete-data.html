Delete Your OverBooked Account or Data

Last updated: August 12, 2026

OverBooked (developer: Jeffery Tkopack) gives you full control over your account and library data. This page explains how to delete either.

Delete a Single Book (Partial Data Deletion)

You can remove any individual book — along with its rating, review, tags, and reading history — without affecting the rest of your account.

  1. Open OverBooked and go to your Library
  2. Tap the book you want to remove
  3. Tap the delete (trash) icon in the top right of the Book Detail screen
  4. Confirm the deletion

This is immediate and cannot be undone.

Delete Your Entire Account

Deleting your account permanently removes your email/login, your entire library, all ratings and reviews, tags, reading dates, and Focus Mode session history. Your account will not exist afterward.

  1. Open OverBooked and sign in
  2. Tap the Account icon
  3. Scroll to the "Danger zone" section
  4. Tap "Delete Account" and confirm

This is immediate and cannot be undone.

Don't Have the App Installed?

If you'd like your account and data deleted but no longer have OverBooked installed, email us using the address below with the subject line "Delete My Account," from the same email address associated with your account. We'll process the request and confirm once it's complete.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account email and login credentials
  • Every book in your library, along with ratings, reviews, tags, and reading dates
  • Your Focus Mode reading session history
  • Your profile icon selection

Nothing is retained after deletion — there is no grace period or backup copy kept.
