Privacy Policy for OverBooked

Last updated: August 11, 2026

OverBooked ("the app," "we," "our") is a personal reading tracker app. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how it's stored.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: if you choose to create an account, we collect your email address and a password (stored securely — we never see or store your password in plain text; it's handled by our authentication provider, Supabase).
  • Your reading library: the books you add, their titles/authors/genres, your ratings, written reviews, tags, reading status, and the dates you started or finished a book.
  • Reading session data: if you use Focus Mode, we log the duration of each reading session so the app can show you reading-time stats and pace estimates.
  • Profile customization: the profile icon you select is saved to your account. Your chosen app background theme and font are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers.

How Your Information Is Used

Your data is used solely to provide the app's functionality to you: tracking your reading, showing your stats and yearly "Wrapped" summary, syncing your library across devices you sign into, and letting you search for books to add.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising. OverBooked does not display ads.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — our backend provider. It stores your account and library data securely in a database, and handles authentication. Supabase's own privacy practices are described at supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google Books API — used only when you search for a book to add. Your search text is sent to Google to retrieve matching book results; no personal account information is shared with this request.

Data Security

Your library data is protected by database-level access rules (Row Level Security) that ensure only you can read or modify your own reading data. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

Your Choices

  • You can use OverBooked without creating an account — a temporary guest profile is created automatically, though its data won't sync across devices or survive a reinstall.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all of your library data — books, ratings, reviews, and reading history — at any time from the Account screen in the app. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • For anything not covered by the in-app deletion, you can also contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

OverBooked is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
